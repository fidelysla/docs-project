# Inglés Gramática

## 1 How much vs How many

Se usa “how much” y “how many” para preguntar por cantidades.

### How many - Sustantivos contables (countable nouns)

Se usa con cosas que puedes contar (tienen plural).

`How many + plural noun + ... ?`

- How many books do you have?

- How many students are in the class?

- How many apples did she buy?

Sustantivos contables:

* book / books
* car / cars
* apple / apples
* people

### How much - Sustantivos incontables (uncountable nouns)

Se usa con cosas que no puedes contar individualmente.

`How much + uncountable noun + ... ?`

- How much water do you drink?
- How much money do you need?
- How much sugar is there?

Sustantivos incontables:

* water
* money
* sugar
* milk
* information

!!! note "“How much” también para precio 💲"

    Cuando preguntamos el precio:

    - How much is this shirt?
    - How much are these shoes?

## 2 Adverbs of Frequency and Frequency Expressions

| Característica             | **Adverbs of Frequency**                                | **Frequency Expressions**                      |
| -------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| **Qué son**                | Palabras únicas que indican con qué frecuencia (how often) ocurre algo | Frases o grupos de palabras que indican frecuencia |
| **Posición en la oración** | Antes del verbo principal (después del verbo "to be")      | Generalmente al inicio o al final de la oración    |
| **Flexibilidad**           | Menos flexibles en su posición                             | Más flexibles en su posición                       |
| **Ejemplos**               | always, usually, often, sometimes, rarely, never           | every day, once a week, three times a month        |


### Ejemplos

**Adverbio de Frecuencia**

- They are **usually** on holiday in July. (Ellos usualmente están de vacaciones en julio).
- He is **often** ready for work at 6 am. (Él a menudo está listo para el trabajo a las 6 am).
- She **always** drinks coffee in the morning.
- He **usually** takes the bus to work. 
- They **often** go hiking on weekends.
- I **sometimes** forget my keys. 
- We **rarely** eat fast food. (Raramente comemos comida rápida.)
- He **never** smokes. 

**Expresión de Frecuencia**

- I go to the gym **every day**. 
- She calls her mom **once a week**.
- We have a meeting **twice a month**.
- They travel abroad **three times a year**.
- He runs *every other day*. 
- I have yoga class **on Mondays**.
- **Every now and then**, I like to cook a special dinner.
- I watch old movies **every now and then**.

**Consejo clave:** Los adverbios de frecuencia van en el *medio* de la oración, mientras que las expresiones de frecuencia van al *inicio o al final*.


## 3 Linkes and Dislikes

`Not/Keen on`

- I'm not keen on Mondays
- I'm keen on science fiction movies.

`Can't stand`

- I can't stand the heat and the humidity.

`Really in/interesting in/into`

- I'm really interested in learning new programming languages.
- I'm really into white cats.
- I'm really into science fiction movies.

`Really love`

- I really love movies about knights.

`Don't mind`

- I don't mind washing my clothes.

`I prefer`

- I prefer quiet places to crowded places.

`Quite like`

- I quite like traveling by train.

`My favourite __ is __`

- My favourite hobbies are playing video games and watching football.

## 4 Who, Which/That, Where

**¿Qué son los pronombres relativos?**

Son palabras que conectan una cláusula subordinada con un sustantivo o pronombre en la oración principal, añadiendo información sobre él.

### WHO

Se usa para referirse a **personas**.

**Usos:**
- Identifica o describe a una persona de la que ya se habló
- Puede reemplazar a he, she, they dentro de la cláusula

**Ejemplos:**
- The woman **who** lives next door is a doctor.
- The students **who** studied hard passed the exam. robaron el examen.
- I have a friend **who** speaks five languages.

### WHICH / THAT

Se usa para referirse a **cosas, animales o ideas**.

**Usos:**

- **Which** se usa para cosas y animales, generalmente en cláusulas no esenciales (con coma)
- **That** se usa para cosas y animales en cláusulas esenciales (sin coma)

**Ejemplos con Which:**

- The book, **which** I bought yesterday, is very interesting.
- Her car, **which** is red, is very fast. 

**Ejemplos con That:**

- The movie **that** we watched last night was amazing.
- This is the dress **that** I want to buy.

### WHERE

Se usa para referirse a **lugares**.

**Usos:**

- Describe o identifica un lugar específico
- Reemplaza expresiones como "in which" o "at which"

**Ejemplos:**

- The city **where** I was born is very small.
- That is the restaurant **where** we had our first date.
- I want to visit the town **where** my grandmother grew up.

### Resumen rápido

| Pronombre | Se usa para | Ejemplo corto |
|---|---|---|
| **Who** | Personas | The man **who** called you |
| **Which** | Cosas/animales (no esencial) | The book, **which** is old |
| **That** | Cosas/animales (esencial) | The car **that** I drive |
| **Where** | Lugares | The park **where** we met |


**Consejo clave:** Si puedes reemplazar la palabra por *él, ella o ellos* → usa **who**. Si puedes reemplazarla por *eso o ello* → usa **which/that**. Si es un lugar → usa **where**.
