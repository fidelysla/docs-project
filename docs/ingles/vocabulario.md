# Vocabulario

- `Damp`: Wet
- `Humid`: Humidity/Moisture/Liquid Vapor
- `Mild`: Cloudy/Temperature that are neither extremely hot nor very cold
- `Pleasant`: Good/Enjoyable/Sunny
- `Showers`: Cloudy/rain

- `Hike`: Walking in the countryside
- `Slope`: The side of a mountain or hill
- `Skiing`: Moving on skis

- `Rubbish`: Trash
- `Stall`: Shop/stand/table
- `Dull`: Boring
- `Pedestrian`: People in Street
- `Oak`: Roble
